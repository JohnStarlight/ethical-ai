## Improvement Actions

Component interaction can get really complicated, really fast. So my first course of action would be to read and understand a small project and how its parts interact with each other.

Second course of action would be to build something similar to experience by first hand how these interactions work and why they are beneficial. This "why" is the most crucial part since I'm not sure about it, other than the fact that modularity makes workflow more... fluid, easy to deal with. So I need to specify if there are other merits to it as well.

Having that "why" understood will give me motivation to move to my third course of action, which would be a more complex task.
