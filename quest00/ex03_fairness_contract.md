# Fair AI Use Contract

## I will use AI when

I am encountering something for the first time and I need it explained.

I have tried solving a problem myself first.

I need to understand why or how something works.

I want to explore alternative approaches or edge cases I might've missed.

I have solved the same problem many times and it offers no more gains to do it one more time (menial tasks).

## I will NOT use AI when

I haven't tried solving the problem on my own.

I am completing an exam or assessment.

I can solve something on my own with reasonable effort.

I am learning from the process of repeating an already known or previously solved task.

## I know I'm using AI fairly when

I can explain mine or an AI's code without the AI's help.

I could solve a similar problem independently.

I feel more confident in my own understanding.

I am able to correct the output of an AI.
